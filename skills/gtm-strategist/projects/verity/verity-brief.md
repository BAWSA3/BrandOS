# Verity — Company Brief

## what verity is

Verity is a multi-asset RWA (Real World Asset) marketplace that enables investors to discover, evaluate, and invest in tokenized real-world assets — including real estate, commodities, private credit, treasuries, and other alternative assets.

## stage

- product: built, pre-launch
- revenue: pre-revenue
- users: none yet (planning GTM for market entry)
- timeline: Q2 2026 launch

## what makes verity different

**1. curated asset access**
Verity doesn't list everything. the marketplace curates unique asset classes and deal flow that other RWA platforms don't offer. the supply side IS part of the moat — exclusive or hard-to-access assets that give investors a reason to come to Verity specifically.

**2. institutional-grade infrastructure & compliance**
built with compliance from day one — not bolted on after. KYC/AML, regulatory alignment, institutional-grade security. this matters for two reasons:
- institutions won't touch a platform without it
- retail investors increasingly want to know their money is on a legit, regulated platform (post-FTX trust deficit)

**the combination**: unique assets you can't get elsewhere + the trust infrastructure to know it's real. "access + assurance."

## target segments

### segment 1: retail investors (volume play)
- crypto-native or crypto-curious individuals
- interested in diversifying beyond pure crypto into "real" assets
- may be new to RWA but understand tokenization conceptually
- entry point: smaller investments, fractional ownership
- channels: crypto twitter, TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, YouTube
- motivation: portfolio diversification, yield, "own real things on-chain"

### segment 2: institutional / accredited investors (TVL play)
- family offices, funds, accredited investors
- looking for alternative asset exposure with blockchain settlement
- care about compliance, reporting, liquidity, and track record
- entry point: larger allocations, possibly exclusive asset classes
- channels: direct outreach, conferences, partner networks, LinkedIn
- motivation: alpha, diversification, blockchain efficiency, reporting

## competitive landscape

**direct competitors (RWA-native):**

| Platform | Focus | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|----------|-------|-----------|------------|
| Ondo Finance | tokenized treasuries, money market | massive TVL ($600M+), institutional credibility | narrow asset focus (mostly treasuries) |
| RealT | tokenized real estate | established, real properties, dividend yield | US real estate only, limited asset types |
| Centrifuge | private credit / structured finance | deep DeFi integration, MakerDAO partnership | complex UX, not retail-friendly |
| Maple Finance | institutional lending / credit | institutional focus, yield products | credit risk, institutional-only |
| Goldfinch | emerging market credit | unique asset class, social impact angle | narrow focus, emerging market risk |

**verity's positioning opportunity:**
most competitors are single-asset-class or single-segment. Verity's multi-asset + dual-segment approach is the gap. nobody is doing "the marketplace for all RWAs" with both retail accessibility and institutional compliance. the risk: being seen as "a little bit of everything" instead of "the best at one thing."

## revenue model

1. **transaction fees** — take rate on buy/sell transactions on the marketplace
2. **listing / issuance fees** — asset issuers pay to tokenize and list
3. **premium tiers** — institutional access, advanced analytics, priority allocation, API access

## constraints

2. **TVL (total value locked)** — capital deployed on platform
3. **revenue** — proof the business model works
