# GTM Strategy Document Template

use this structure when producing strategy mode output. adapt section depth based on what the client needs — not every engagement requires full TAM analysis.

---

## 1. executive summary

```
CLIENT: [company name]
STAGE: [pre-revenue / seed / growth / scale / enterprise]
PRIMARY GTM MOTION: [PLG / sales-led / community-led / hybrid]
FRAMEWORKS APPLIED: [JTBD, AARRR, Category Design, Blue Ocean — whichever were used]
KEY INSIGHT: [1-2 sentences — the single most important strategic finding]
```

→ a decision-maker should be able to read this block and know the recommendation.

## 2. situation assessment

synthesize the discovery findings into a narrative. not a data dump — a story.

structure:
- **what the company does** (1-2 sentences, plain language)
- **where they are now** (stage, revenue, team, traction)
- **what's working** (existing strengths, channels, product advantages)
- **what's not working** (gaps, bottlenecks, strategic risks)
- **the core question** (what this GTM strategy needs to answer)

## 3. market analysis

### total addressable market (TAM / SAM / SOM)

```
TAM: $[X]B — [how calculated: top-down from industry reports or bottom-up from buyer count × ACV]
SAM: $[X]M — [TAM filtered by geography, segment, and use case the client can actually serve]
SOM: $[X]M — [SAM filtered by realistic capture rate in 12-24 months given current resources]
```

→ be honest about SOM. most TAM slides are fantasy. SOM should be defensible.

## 5. positioning

### positioning statement

```
For [target customer]
who [situation or need],
[product name] is a [category]
that [primary benefit / key differentiator].
Unlike [primary alternative],
we [unique advantage].
```

### messaging hierarchy

**level 1 — tagline** (5-8 words, captures the essence)
[tagline]

**level 2 — value proposition** (1-2 sentences, explains the benefit)
[value prop]

**level 3 — proof points** (3-5 supporting claims with evidence)
1. [claim + evidence]
2. [claim + evidence]
3. [claim + evidence]

**level 4 — features** (specific capabilities that deliver the proof points)
- [feature → tied to which proof point]

→ sales and marketing should always lead with level 1-2, go to level 3 when challenged, and level 4 only when asked.

**90-day checkpoint:** at day 90, evaluate:
- are the unit economics working? (LTV:CAC > 3:1 or trending toward it)
- is the primary channel scalable? (can we 3x spend without 3x CAC)
- is retention flattening? (PMF signal)
- decision: double down, pivot the motion, or adjust the ICP
