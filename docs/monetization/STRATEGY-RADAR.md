tweet is `low` confidence; repeated across independent companies is `high`. Plays that
contradict a settled decision in `docs/GTM-OPERATING-PLAN.md` or `docs/LAUNCH-PLAN.md` are
listed with "challenges a decision" and stop there. No paid ads during the launch (LAUNCH-PLAN).
