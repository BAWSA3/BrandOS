## The offer, framed

What it is (unchanged): everything free until Dec 3, no card. The first 100 who add a card
keep Pro at **$29/mo for life** (first charge Dec 3) plus a recorded brand teardown from
Bawsa. After 100, Pro is $39/mo. After Dec 3, a card is needed to try it.

How we say it, in this order of strength:

1. **"Lock it, don't buy it."** Nothing is charged today. Adding a card now only locks the
   price; cancel before Dec 3 and you pay nothing. This removes the main objection to paying
   for something that's free right now.
2. **"For life."** $29 now vs $39 for everyone after the first 100. It never goes up for you.
3. **"Numbered."** Founding member No. 1–100, shown on their Index card and Brand Pass.
   Status, not a discount.
4. **"Bawsa looks at your brand."** The recorded teardown is the most valuable part for your
   audience, and it's capped by your time. Say it plainly: "I'll personally tear down your brand."

**Never:** fake counters, fake deadlines, discounts on top of the founding price. The
counter is the real `FoundingMember` count; Dec 3 is real.

## Campaigns, week by week

Each week has one theme. Every post, email and DM that week points at it.

### Week 0, Oct 23–Nov 1: "They can't see you" (warm-up)

- Problem posts + teardowns (GTM plan phase 1). No offer, no links except "reply 'index'".
- Goal: **50 people asking for an early number.** That's the launch-day list.

### Week 1, Nov 2–8: "Get listed"

- **Nov 2:** launch thread (pinned) → the Index is open → founding offer in the last post.
  Launch email to the list. DM all "index" + "ACCESS" repliers with their link.
- Daily member spotlights (members repost them; that's the loop).
- **Thu Nov 5:** first X Space: "live brand teardowns". Founding members go first.
- **Fri Nov 6:** nudge email to everyone who hasn't joined.
- Target: **200 members, 10 cards.**

### Week 2, Nov 9–15: "Get verified"

- Verified campaign (`x-post-ideas/2026-11-verified-campaign.md`): the badge is Pro-only.
  Hero video "The Check", stamp loop, personalized Verified cards.
- Brand side opens: 5 brand/agency DMs a day; invited brands browse the Index free.
- **Thu Nov 12:** data post from the Index (real aggregate numbers).
- Weekly email (list-wide slot): "what verified means + who got it" (with permission).
- Target: **300 members, 18 cards.**

### Week 3, Nov 16–22: "Get seen"

- "Brands viewed you" is live: post the first real brand views (aggregate: "brands viewed
  {n} Index profiles this week").
- First brand intro made through the Index → story post (both sides agree).
- Weekly email: "brands are browsing. here's what they look at."
- Decide on lever 9 (bring a friend) by Nov 12; ship Nov 16 if yes.
- Target: **400 members, 25 cards.**

### Week 4, Nov 23–Dec 3: "Lock it"

- **Thu Nov 26 (Thanksgiving):** thank-you post naming founding members (with permission).
- **Fri Nov 27 (Black Friday):** "no black friday sale. the founding price is already the
  lowest it will ever be: $29 for life, {n} spots, ends dec 3."
- **Mon Nov 30 (Cyber Monday):** deadline email #1. **Wed Dec 2:** deadline email #2.
- Daily: spots left + one member story ("why i locked it").
- **Thu Dec 3:** first charges. Thank-you post. Switch the site to the paid trial.
- Target: **500 members, 40 cards.**

## Measure

**Every morning:** scans, signups, Index joins, cards added (GTM scorecard).

**Every Sunday (30 min):**

1. Compute the real rates: scan → signup, signup → join, join → card.
2. Find the weakest step and change one thing for it this week (copy, a CTA, the DM).
3. Read `CUSTOMER-VOICE.md`: the words people use go into next week's posts.
4. Write the week's numbers into LAUNCH-PLAN.

**Decision rules:**

- Cards behind target two Sundays in a row → more DMs to segment D, fewer posts.
- Joins high, cards low → the Pro difference isn't visible enough: lead with levers 2–4.
- Joins low → fix the join flow (watch 3 people join on a call) before posting more.
- Spots under 20 left → say so every day; never invent urgency before that.
