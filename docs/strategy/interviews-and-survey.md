## Interview guide: creators (20 min)

1. Tell me about the last time a brand or project reached out to you for a paid post. How did they find you?
2. The last time you _didn't_ get a deal you wanted, what do you think happened?
3. What do you do today to show a brand you're worth it? (media kit, screenshots, DMs, nothing?)
4. You scanned your brand on BrandOS. What were you hoping to find out? What did you do right after?
5. Did you come back? What would make you check it every week?
6. What do you pay for today to grow on X (tools, courses, Premium, ghostwriters)? How much a month?
7. If BrandOS disappeared tomorrow, what would you use instead?
8. _(Last 5 min)_ Founding is $29/mo for life, free until Dec 3. Would you add a card today? Why or why not?

## Interview guide: SMMs / agencies (25 min)

1. How many accounts do you run? What do you report to clients each month, and how long does it take?
2. Walk me through the last time a client asked "is this working?" What did you show them?
3. Which tools do you pay for now (analytics, scheduling, discovery)? Price per month? Who pays, you or the client?
4. Have you ever had to find creators for a client? How did you pick them, and how long did it take?
5. What would a tool have to do to replace one you pay for today?
6. _(Last 5 min)_ Team is $99/mo for several brands, Agency is $249. Which would you need, and what's missing?

## Interview guide: people who pay creators (25 min). This is the test that matters most

1. Tell me about your last creator/KOL campaign. How many creators, what budget, what result?
2. How did you choose those creators? Who made the shortlist, and how long did it take?
3. What went wrong? (fake engagement, wrong audience, no delivery, couldn't measure)
4. What do you pay today to find or vet creators? (agency retainer, Kaito, Favikon, spreadsheets, interns)
5. If you could search a public book of crypto creators, each with a read of their real posts and a track record, what would you search for first?
6. What would you need to see before trusting a score you didn't make?
7. _(Last 5 min, the ask)_ We open the Creator Index on Nov 2. Index access with search and shortlists is $249/mo. Would you sign a letter of intent to start in December at that price? If not, at what price or with what feature would you?

**Pass:** 3 or more of 10 say yes and sign (one paragraph by email is enough). **Fail:** 0 of 10. Then write down exactly why.

## The "very disappointed" survey (Sean Ellis test)

Send to members who used BrandOS at least twice in their first two weeks (target n ≥ 30). Run it between Nov 20 and Dec 1.

1. **How would you feel if you could no longer use BrandOS?**
   - Very disappointed
   - Somewhat disappointed
   - Not disappointed (it isn't really that useful)
   - N/A, I no longer use BrandOS
2. What type of people do you think would most benefit from BrandOS?
3. What is the main benefit you get from BrandOS?
4. How can we improve BrandOS for you?
5. What would you use instead if BrandOS were gone?

**Pass:** at least 40% "very disappointed". **Fail:** under 25%. Question 2's answers tell us who to sell to; question 3's answers become the homepage line.

## Price-range questions (Van Westendorp), added to the pricing poll

Ask separately of creators, SMMs/agencies and brands (one question first: "Which best describes you?"). Answer in $ per month for what BrandOS gives _you_.

1. At what price would BrandOS be **so cheap** you'd doubt it's any good?
2. At what price would it be **a bargain**, a great buy for the money?
3. At what price would it start to feel **expensive**, though you'd still consider it?
4. At what price would it be **too expensive** to consider?

Plot the four answers by group. The range between where "too cheap" crosses "expensive" and where "bargain" crosses "too expensive" is the acceptable price range. Check whether $29/$39 (creators) and $99/$249 (teams, agencies, brands) fall inside it.
