## Financial constraints

- Every build >1 week needs an explicit ROI thesis before starting.
- No paid marketing budget. Growth must be organic or product-led.

## Time constraints
- Solo means context-switching is expensive. Prefer one bet per week.
- April paying-customer goal is the forcing function.
- Strategy sessions happen in claude.ai. Build sessions happen in
  Claude Code. Don't mix them.

## What Claude should NOT recommend
- Full rewrites or "rebuild from scratch" suggestions.
- Hiring, outsourcing, or "get a designer for this."
- Marketplace pivots, platform pivots, any rebrand.
- Adding team members or co-founders.
- Strategies that assume >20hrs/week of focused build time.
- Any product expansion that delays the April paying-customer goal.
- Ideas that require fundraising (not the path right now).

## What Claude SHOULD prefer
- Small, testable bets that ship in a week or less.
- Revenue- or retention-driving work over "nice to have."
- Leveraging existing infrastructure (Supabase, Claude, SocialData,
  Vercel) over new vendor adds.
- Ideas validated by CUSTOMER-VOICE.md over theoretical wins.
- Making what's already shipped BETTER before building new surface area.
