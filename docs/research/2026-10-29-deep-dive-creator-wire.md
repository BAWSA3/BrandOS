# Deep dive: Creator Wire (2026-10-29)

Asked by Bawsa: "my first real competition (i think)". Sources: creatorwire.xyz (page metadata
and the site's own copy, read 2026-10-29), the BrandOS read of @creator_wire (2026-10-29).
A web search found no press or third-party coverage, so every claim below is **their own
claim** unless marked otherwise.

## The answer (5 lines max)

1. Creator Wire is a **crypto KOL campaign workspace** run by people who were a KOL agency:
   vetted creators, approvals, budgets, onchain payouts and automatic reporting in one tool.
2. They already have what our design-partner track is trying to get by Dec 3: brands paying,
   a vetted network ("700+"), and **case studies with real numbers** (Polkadot DeFi TVL
   $27M → $54M in 60 days; a $34 CPM campaign; a 219-creator mindshare program).
3. They are **not** an open index and **not** creator-facing intelligence. Creators apply and
   get accepted or rejected, and their tiers live inside their platform.
4. Biggest risk: they open the network as a public, browsable list or give creators a
   self-view. That's a short step for them.
5. Don't fight them on campaign ops. Be the **open, neutral, creator-owned record** they (and
   every agency) source from, and test whether they're a partner before treating them as a rival.

## What they are (their words, as of 2026-10-29)

- Title: "Creator Campaign Management for Web3 Teams". Description: "the workspace for crypto
  creator campaigns. Source vetted creators, run KOL campaigns, approve deliverables, track
  results, and pay onchain, all in one place."
- Origin: "We ran crypto creator campaigns the manual way for years. This is the system we
  wished existed." App live at app.creatorwire.xyz.
- **Three surfaces**: operators (agencies/teams running campaigns), creators, clients
  ("one branded link for your client, their logo next to yours", i.e. white-label for agencies).
- **Workflow**: "six steps from first shortlist to everyone paid": shortlist → brief →
  approvals "with live budget rollups" → deliverables → reporting (impressions, CPM, posts,
  engagement "pulled live from the dashboard") → "one payment covers every creator, with
  optional instant payouts" (onchain). Self-serve bounty campaigns for quick pushes.
- **Vetting**: "How we vet 700+ crypto creators". "Tiered and benchmarked with real
  engagement data". The pitch is aimed at our exact pain: "Every crypto marketer has been
  burned by a creator with big follower numbers and dead engagement." Tiers are recalculated
  as engagement changes.
- **Creator side**: apply, "we review fit", accepted creators get "briefs, transparent rates,
  and instant payouts". A creator competition ("Call the Outcome", Season 01) with badges.
- **Turtle distributor program** (run with Turtle): creators, newsletters and community leads
  share vetted DeFi deals and "earn a cut of every deposit it brings, credited to you onchain",
  plus a retainer for steady delivery. Free, non-exclusive.
- **Logos and names on the site** (relationship not stated or verified; may be clients, case
  studies or Turtle's backers): Polkadot, ZAMA, OKX, CoinGecko, Mantle, Cardano, Internet
  Computer, 0G, Katana, Limitless, Neo, Supra; Amber Group, FalconX, GSR, Auros, Fasanara,
  Chorus One, Bodhi Ventures. **Inference:** the market-maker/fund names are most likely Turtle
  backers ("Backed by serious capital"), not Creator Wire clients.
- **Pricing**: "Tiers and transparent pricing" is a section heading; no prices found in the
  site copy. Gap.

## How they could become a direct competitor (most likely first)

1. **Open the network**: a public "vetted creators" directory or leaderboard. They already
   have tiers, a review queue and badges ("badges are minted here"). That's the Creator Index
   with a closed door instead of an opt-in.
2. **A creator self-view**: "think your numbers hold up?" is already their creator CTA. A
   free "see your tier" page would compete head-on with the BrandOS scan for creators.
3. **Receipts at scale**: every campaign they run logs onchain payments and delivery. That's
   the track record we're starting with `IndexDeal`, except theirs is full and verified by money
   moving. **This is the most serious overlap.**
4. **Selling the benchmarks**: tiers sold as a vetting product to other agencies.
5. **Beyond crypto**: unlikely soon; everything they have (onchain payouts, Turtle) is crypto.

## So what for BrandOS

- **Our design-partner pitch must not be "campaign management"**. They do that better, with
  payouts and reporting. Ours is the step before them: "who's real and who fits, in public,
  owned by the creator". The design-partner section already rules out running campaigns; keep it.
- **Test a partnership before treating them as a rival.** Their creators must "apply and
  prove their numbers"; a BrandOS read + Index number could be that proof, and the Index could be
  a sourcing pool for operators. Worth one DM from Bawsa: "how do you find creators beyond the
  700?" (same brand-DM question). **Inference**, untested.
- **Copy their proof format.** Case studies with real numbers, "including the ones still in
  flight", CPM published, "how we vet" posts. `docs/case-studies/TEMPLATE.md` already asks for
  this; their site is the bar.
- **Neutrality becomes the line in the sand.** They're an operator earning from clients and
  Turtle deposits. BrandOS is the record no one pays to be ranked in. Say it plainly in brand posts.
- **Move the "receipts" story up the order.** They already turn delivery data into proof.
  The Nov 12 "brands confirm a deal" step (#211 → mig 046) is what keeps BrandOS's record from
  looking thin next to theirs.

## Challenges a decision (only if the evidence does)

with case studies. **Inference:** pitch the agencies and teams that _aren't_ on a workspace
yet, or that need creators beyond a closed network, not Creator Wire's own clients.
