it. Kept by the `market-research` agent. Every price and number has a source
and an "as of" date in the company's section.

(influencer marketplaces, creator analytics, media kits) · **substitute**
(agencies, spreadsheets, KOL groups) · **platform** (X or another big platform
shipping it).

## Profiles

### X Creator Connect (business.x.com)

- **What it is:** X's own AI-matched creator program for brands. It is built on xAI, and X runs outreach, content and distribution.
- **Who pays / price (as of 2026-10-23):** Brands, through X. Pricing not disclosed.
- **Core features:**
  - Ranks creators against a brief: campaign goals, audience interest, real-time trends.
  - Finds "lookalike" creators.
  - Brand approval at each stage.
  - Paired with Trend Genius for creative assets.
- **Positioning (their words):** "Creator Connect is actually much more designed for those rising creators, those commercial niche creators." (Mitchell Smith, X)
- **Traction:** Example campaigns for a premium laptop maker (tech creators who follow F1) and a horror film. Soccer was planned for the World Cup. No numbers given.
- **Weak spots (coverage says):**
  - "The company is a bit vague on the mechanics of it" (Social Media Today).
  - Creators can't self-onboard, opt out, or apply (DesignRush).
  - No rollout update found after May 2026.
- **vs BrandOS:**
  - **They win:** X's first-party data, X's ad sales team, brand budgets already on X.
  - **BrandOS wins:** creators can opt in and see their own read; the read is public and browsable; it doesn't need an ad-buy relationship.
  - **Could they ship the Index next month?** Technically yes. Their design choice so far is managed and brand-only, the opposite of an open index.
- **Sources (read 2026-10-23):** [Yahoo/THR](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/x-making-major-creator-push-155441997.html), [Marketing Week](https://www.marketingweek.com/x-creator-connect/), [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/x-announces-creator-driven-ad-partnership/820540/), [DesignRush](https://news.designrush.com/x-launches-creator-connect-ai-tool), [Creator Handbook](https://www.creatorhandbook.net/x-launches-new-creator-sponsorship-marketplace/)

### X profile trust features

- **What it is:** Free signals X builds into profiles and posts.
  - "About this account" (country or region, Nov 2025).
  - Native "Paid Partnership" label (about Mar 1, 2026).
  - Grok "More about this account" summary (Premium, desktop, as of Jul 2024).
- **Who pays / price:** Free. The Grok summary is Premium only.
- **Positioning (their words):** Bier: undisclosed promotions "lead people to distrust the content they read on X."
- **Policy context:**
  - Jan 15, 2026: X revoked API access for apps that pay users to post (the InfoFi ban).
  - Whether crypto is allowed in paid partnerships is contradictory across sources (Mar–May 2026). EU, UK and Australia are excluded either way. **Unverified; see the brief's Gaps.**
- **vs BrandOS:**
  - **They win:** free, native, and seen on every profile.
  - **BrandOS wins:** none of it says whether a creator fits a brand.
  - **Risk:** any "Verified = real account" badge loses value as X ships more of these.
- **Sources (read 2026-10-23):** [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/03/02/x-lifts-cryptocurrency-and-gambling-bans-for-paid-promotions/), [SpendNode](https://www.spendnode.io/blog/x-paid-partnership-policy-bans-crypto-influencer-promotions-ads-still-allowed/), [CryptoAdventure](https://cryptoadventure.com/x-paid-partnerships-policy-blocks-sponsored-crypto-promotions/), [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/354736/elon-musks-x-bans-access-infofi-crypto-projects-ai-slop-backlash), [Social Media Today 2024](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/x-rolls-out-new-ways-to-use-its-grok-ai-chatbot/722351/), Editorialge (via search)

### Kaito (kaito.ai)

- **What it is:** Crypto attention and mindshare data company. After X's InfoFi ban it runs:
  - **Studio** (Feb 2026 beta): a curated, tiered brand/creator marketplace.
  - **Katalyst** (Jul 29, 2026): pays creators on outcomes.
- **Who pays / price (as of 2026-10-23):** Brands fund campaigns and pay creators in digital assets. Kaito Pro from **$833/mo** (CoinGecko, updated May 7, 2026).
- **Core features:**
  - Brands post campaigns; creators apply and quote a price.
  - Kaito screens creators on followers, reach and impressions.
  - Covers X, YouTube and TikTok, in crypto, finance, AI and other verticals.
  - Katalyst attributes clicks, sign-ups and deposits to creators, with 80% of the pool going to creators.
- **Positioning (their words):** "Where creators and brands grow together."
- **Traction:** 16 brand partners at Studio launch. X supplies data "under a data agreement" (OurCryptoTalk). Creator counts and payouts not published.
- **Weak spots:**
  - Commenters say it looks like "a closed marketing agency, where rewards may keep going to the same group of creators."
  - Creators report reward delays.
  - Rejection reasons aren't disclosed.
- **vs BrandOS:**
  - **They win:** brand relationships, crypto data depth, money flowing to creators, a deal with X.
  - **BrandOS wins:** open to any creator who opts in; a public brand read rather than mindshare; doesn't require a tier.
  - **Could they ship the Index next month?** Yes. They have the data and the brand list. They chose curation, but an open "Studio directory" would be easy for them.
- **Sources (read 2026-10-23):** [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/learn/what-is-kaito-earn-yap-points), [kaito.ai/studio](https://www.kaito.ai/studio), [OurCryptoTalk](https://ourcryptotalk.com/news/kaito-katalyst-creator-rewards), [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/354736/elon-musks-x-bans-access-infofi-crypto-projects-ai-slop-backlash), KuCoin community (via search)

### Favikon (favikon.com)

- **What it is:** A creator scoring and discovery platform across 9 networks including X. It publishes free public rankings by niche and country.
- **Who pays / price (as of 2026-10-23):**
  - Brands: Free, Core €159/mo, Pro €239/mo, Enterprise from €499/mo. Annual prices are shown higher (€199 / €299); unclear.
  - Creators: Creator **€29/mo** (14-day card trial), Creator Plus €99/mo.
- **Core features:**
  - Authority, Influence and Authenticity scores. Authenticity is built for LinkedIn and X.
  - Weekly top-200 rankings in "600+ niches and 100+ countries."
  - "Top 20 Crypto Influencers on X" lists with an "X Score."
  - Lookalikes, outreach, Claude/ChatGPT integration, and a shareable Spotlight profile for creators.
- **Positioning (their words):** "The AI Infrastructure for Influence."
- **Traction (they say):** "10M+ creators across 9 networks," 6,000+ brand and agency teams (homepage); 10,000+ on the pricing page.
- **Weak spots (reviews say):**
  - Trustpilot: 41 reviews, about 4.1. A paying LinkedIn creator says real fast growth was penalized as inorganic, and support felt copy-paste.
  - Capterra: 4.6/5 from 34.
- **vs BrandOS:**
  - **They win:** multi-platform, an existing brand customer base, public rankings already indexed by Google.
  - **BrandOS wins:** X- and crypto-native; brand-fit read and archetype rather than influence; opt-in membership rather than being ranked without consent.
  - **Could they ship the Index next month?** Yes, most easily of anyone. Rankings plus a creator plan plus a Spotlight profile is most of it already.
- **Sources (read 2026-10-23):** [favikon.com](https://www.favikon.com/), [pricing](https://www.favikon.com/pricing); via search: [crypto on X list](https://www.favikon.com/blog/top-crypto-influencers-x-worldwide), [Trustpilot](https://pt.trustpilot.com/review/favikon.com), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1012035/favikon)

### TwitterScore (twitterscore.io)

- **What it is:** A score for crypto X accounts, 0–1000, by follower quality.
- **Who pays / price (as of 2026-10-23):**
  - Consumer PRO **$19/mo** or $228/yr, 7-day card trial. One checkout screen showed $29.
  - API: Basic $49, Standard $99, Advanced $199, Enterprise custom.
- **Core features:** Smart Mentions and Smart Follows, follower categories, Top 100, trending, bulk score check (50 accounts per call), Chrome extension.
- **Positioning (their words):** "rates every crypto Twitter/X account from 0 to 1000 by follower quality, not follower count."
- **Traction (they say):** "Trusted By 300+ VCs & Crypto Projects."
- **Weak spots:** Built to spot projects and "gems," not to vet creators for brands. Pricing is inconsistent across pages. No reviews found.
- **vs BrandOS:**
  - **They win:** a known crypto-native score, plus an API for agencies.
  - **BrandOS wins:** a read on brand fit and voice, not who follows you; a creator-facing coaching loop.
  - **Could they ship the Index next month?** A ranked list, yes. Opt-in profiles with a brand read, unlikely.
- **Sources (read 2026-10-23):** [twitterscore.io](https://twitterscore.io/), [API prices](https://twitterscore.io/api/prices)

### Ethos Network (ethos.network)

- **What it is:** Onchain credibility scores for crypto people and X accounts, built from staked vouches, reviews and slashes, on Base.
- **Who pays / price (as of 2026-10-23):** No subscription seen. Users stake ETH or WHUF to vouch.
- **Core features:** Credibility score; Chrome extension showing scores on X, Farcaster and OpenSea (v1.13.5, Mar 9, 2026); reviews and slashing.
- **Positioning (their words):** "Reputation & credibility for crypto, driven by peer-to-peer reviews & secured by staked Ethereum."
- **Traction:** No live counts shown on the app page.
- **Weak spots:** Critics flag vouch-for-vouch inflation, a "circle jerk" effect (Decrypt, via search).
- **vs BrandOS:**
  - **They win:** crypto-native trust with real money at stake.
  - **BrandOS wins:** says something about content and brand fit, and needs no wallet or social graph to get a read.
  - **Could they ship the Index next month?** Not a brand directory, though a "top credible creators" view is plausible.
- **Sources (read 2026-10-23):** [app.ethos.network](https://app.ethos.network/), [ethos.network](https://www.ethos.network/); via search: [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/309231/ethos-network-on-chain-reputation-trust), [Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/token-transparency/filing/ethos)

### Lever.io (lever.io)

- **What it is:** A Web3 KOL marketplace plus a done-for-you service, covering X, YouTube and TikTok.
- **Who pays / price (as of 2026-10-23):** Brands. Custom plans; no prices published.
- **Core features:** Search, sort and filter KOLs, or get matched; claims checks against creators who "spoofed their numbers."
- **Positioning (their words):** "First Data Driven KOL Marketplace."
- **Traction (they say):** 500+ verified Web3 influencers, 300+ brands, 24-hour booking.
- **Weak spots:** FORKOFF (a competitor) says the vetting criteria are proprietary, and escrow doesn't catch bot-inflated accounts.
- **vs BrandOS:**
  - **They win:** they transact; brands can book.
  - **BrandOS wins:** a larger open roster potential and a public brand read.
  - **Could they ship the Index next month?** They have a marketplace already. The gap is the brand read.
- **Sources (read 2026-10-23):** [lever.io](https://lever.io/), [FORKOFF](https://forkoff.xyz/blog/influencer-marketing/best-crypto-kol-marketing-platforms-2026)

### Crypto KOL agencies: KolHQ, Growgami, Coinbound and others

- **What it is:** Managed KOL campaigns with private rosters. This is the default substitute.
- **Who pays / price (as of 2026-10-23):**
  - Projects pay. FORKOFF lists most agencies at about $5K+/mo or by quote.
  - Coinzilla sold fixed packages at €1,999 / €4,999 / €9,999 (the Coinzilla page now 404s).
  - Growgami's quoted creator rates: micro $500–2K, mid $2K–10K, top $10K–100K+ per post.
- **Core features:**
  - KolHQ: "vetted list of 5000+ crypto KOLs by niche, platform and price," shortlisted in 48 hours.
  - Growgami: "G-Score" covering real engagement, audience, past conversions, consistency and brand safety.
- **Positioning (their words):** KolHQ: "a crypto KOL marketing agency trusted by top web3 brands."
- **Traction (they say):** KolHQ 300+ projects, 400+ campaigns. Growgami 1,000+ creators.
- **Weak spots:** Across the FORKOFF comparison, the recurring flag is "no published vetting method." FORKOFF itself is an agency.
- **vs BrandOS:**
  - **They win:** they deliver campaigns end to end.
  - **BrandOS wins:** transparent reads and a creator-owned profile.
  - **Inference:** agencies are as likely to be Index **customers** as competitors.
- **Sources (read 2026-10-23):** [FORKOFF](https://forkoff.xyz/blog/influencer-marketing/best-crypto-kol-marketing-platforms-2026), [KolHQ](https://www.kolhq.com/), [Growgami](https://growgami.com/learn/crypto-kol-marketing)

### Passionfroot (passionfroot.me)

- **What it is:** It started as creator storefronts and media kits for sponsorships (X, newsletters, LinkedIn, YouTube and more). It is now an AI agent ("Zest") that runs B2B creator campaigns for brands.
- **Who pays / price (as of 2026-10-23):** Brands. Earlier fees reported as 15% (network bookings) and 5% (direct bookings), via search. Creators "start for free."
- **Core features:** Creator media kit with rates and a booking form, a sponsor inbox, invoicing and payouts; for brands, Zest plus a "Creator Graph" of pricing and performance.
- **Positioning (their words):** "The Agentic Marketing OS for the AI era."
- **Traction:** $15M Series A (Insight Partners, Jul 22, 2026); revenue up 13x in a year; at least $10M paid to creators in 18 months; ElevenLabs, Figma and Replit as clients (TechCrunch).
- **Weak spots:** No reviews checked this run.
- **vs BrandOS:**
  - **They win:** money moves through them; B2B brand demand.
  - **BrandOS wins:** crypto and X-native, and a brand read rather than a rate card.
  - **Could they ship the Index next month?** They could add crypto; not their focus.
- **Sources (read 2026-10-23):** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/22/passionfroot-raises-15m-to-expand-its-b2b-creator-marketplace-to-the-us/), [passionfroot.me](https://www.passionfroot.me/), [creators](https://www.passionfroot.me/creators)

### SuperX (superx.so)

- **What it is:** An X growth and scheduling suite sold to creators. It competes with BrandOS's growth tools, not the Index.
- **Who pays / price (as of 2026-10-23):** Creators. Pro $49, Advanced $49 at launch price (normally $99), Ultra $199.
- **Core features:** Scheduling, an AI writer, auto-DMs, "Signal Agents" for leads, a viral posts library, API/MCP.
- **Positioning (their words):** "Everything you need to grow on 𝕏."
- **Traction (they say):** 11,294+ creators.
- **Weak spots:** Nothing on brand reads or brand deals.
- **vs BrandOS:** they win on writing and posting tools. BrandOS wins on brand read and getting found. Not an Index threat.
- **Sources (read 2026-10-23):** [pricing](https://superx.so/pricing)

### Addressable (addressable.io)

- **What it is:** Wallet-targeted ads for crypto on X, Reddit and the web, pitched as the replacement for KOLs.
- **Who pays / price:** Crypto brands. Not published.
- **Positioning (their words):** co-founder: "Most KOLs won't write about crypto anymore. Ads are the most promising way."
- **Traction (they say):** 190M wallets mapped to 23M social profiles.
- **vs BrandOS:** a substitute for the whole KOL budget. If its "KOLs are a liability" story wins, there is less brand demand for the Index.
- **Sources (read 2026-10-23):** [Addressable blog, Mar 16, 2026](https://www.addressable.io/blog/the-death-of-the-web3-kol)
