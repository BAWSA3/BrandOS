# Competitors: full first sweep, four rings (2026-10-23)

## The answer (5 lines max)

1. X built the brand side itself. **Creator Connect** (May 2026) uses xAI to match brands with "rising" and niche creators, and X runs the deal. It is managed and brand-only: creators can't apply or opt in.
2. Kaito left open leaderboards after X's Jan 2026 InfoFi ban. It now runs **Kaito Studio** (curated, tiered brand/creator marketplace) and **Katalyst** (pays creators on outcomes). It now has a data agreement with X. In crypto, it is the nearest competitor.
3. **Favikon** already sells nearly the same Pro offer: public creator rankings, including "Top crypto influencers on X", an authenticity score, and a €29/mo creator plan with a shareable profile.
4. What nobody sells yet: an **opt-in, public, browsable book of X creators with a brand-fit read**. Everyone else scores influence or authenticity, or they gate access through a closed, managed marketplace.
5. Watch: whether X allows **paid crypto partnerships** at all. The sources disagree (details below). Verify this before the brand side opens on Nov 9.

## Findings

### Ring 4 first: what X has shipped (biggest risk)

- **Creator Connect, announced May 18, 2026.** X calls it an AI system that picks creators for brand campaigns using xAI, campaign goals, real-time trends and audience interest. X handles outreach, content and distribution, and the brand approves each step. X's head of content partnerships, Mitchell Smith, said it is "much more designed for those rising creators, those commercial niche creators." Pricing, markets and fit criteria were not disclosed. ([Yahoo/THR](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/x-making-major-creator-push-155441997.html), [Marketing Week](https://www.marketingweek.com/x-creator-connect/), [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/x-announces-creator-driven-ad-partnership/820540/)). Confidence: **high** (several outlets, same facts).
  - DesignRush says creators "can't self-onboard, opt out of being surfaced, or apply directly to specific campaigns." ([DesignRush](https://news.designrush.com/x-launches-creator-connect-ai-tool)). Confidence: **medium** (one outlet's reading; X's own post was behind a paywall/login for us).
  - I found no rollout update after May. Social Media Today noted "The company is a bit vague on the mechanics of it."
  - **Inference:** this proves the problem is real (X itself says brands can't find niche creators). It does not solve it from the creator side. A creator still can't raise their hand to X. BrandOS's opt-in and the public brand read are the gap.
- **"Year of the creator" moves.** In Jan 2026 X doubled its creator revenue-sharing pool, and in March 2026 it revamped Creator Subscriptions. ([TechCrunch](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/x-revamps-creator-subscriptions-with-new-features-like-exclusive-threads-and-shareable-cards/)). Confidence: medium.
- **Native "Paid Partnership" label, from about March 1, 2026.** It replaces #ad. Nikita Bier said undisclosed promotions "lead people to distrust the content they read on X." ([Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/03/02/x-lifts-cryptocurrency-and-gambling-bans-for-paid-promotions/), [SpendNode](https://www.spendnode.io/blog/x-paid-partnership-policy-bans-crypto-influencer-promotions-ads-still-allowed/)). Confidence: **high**.
- **Crypto paid partnerships: conflicting reports.**
  - Dataconomy, Etherworld and Invezz (via search) say crypto was taken off the prohibited list and is allowed with the label. CCN and Invezz headlines say EU, UK and Australia are still excluded. ([Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/03/02/x-lifts-cryptocurrency-and-gambling-bans-for-paid-promotions/), [Etherworld](https://etherworld.co/x-policy-update-opens-door-to-crypto-paid-promotions/))
  - SpendNode (updated May 8, 2026), CryptoAdventure and Addressable say the opposite: crypto "is no longer eligible for paid partnership promotion", and only X Ads remain open. ([SpendNode](https://www.spendnode.io/blog/x-paid-partnership-policy-bans-crypto-influencer-promotions-ads-still-allowed/), [CryptoAdventure](https://cryptoadventure.com/x-paid-partnerships-policy-blocks-sponsored-crypto-promotions/), [Addressable](https://www.addressable.io/blog/the-death-of-the-web3-kol))
  - X's own policy page returned 403 to us. Confidence: **low** on the current rule. See Gaps.
- **InfoFi ban, Jan 15, 2026.** Bier: "We will no longer allow apps that reward users for posting on X." X revoked API access for these apps. ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/354736/elon-musks-x-bans-access-infofi-crypto-projects-ai-slop-backlash)). Confidence: **high**.
  - Kaito sunset Yaps, Cookie DAO wound down Snaps, and Xeet said its campaigns had stopped. KAITO fell about 17% and COOKIE about 15% that day (per search summaries of [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/kaito-plummets-as-x-bans-infofi-rewards) and [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/x-bans-incentivized-posting-apps-prompting-shakeup-in-crypto-engagement-platforms/)). Confidence: medium.
  - **Inference:** X will cut off whole categories of third-party X apps when they hurt the timeline. BrandOS doesn't pay for posting, but it reads X through SocialData. A category ban like this is a live supplier risk.
- **"About this account", Nov 2025.** Profiles now show the country or region an account is based in. Bier framed it as an authenticity tool. (Search summary of [Editorialge](https://editorialge.com/?p=259992)). Confidence: medium.
  - **Inference:** X is shipping free authenticity signals into the profile. A "Verified" badge that only says "this account is real" gets weaker every time X does this.
- **Grok "More about this account."** An AI summary on profiles: what the account posts about, its age and follower count. Premium only, desktop only, as of **July 2024**. ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/x-rolls-out-new-ways-to-use-its-grok-ai-chatbot/722351/)). Confidence: medium, but old. Current state not checked.

### Ring 1: direct (crypto KOL discovery and scoring on X)

- **Kaito.**
  - Yaps ended Jan 15, 2026. Studio went to beta in Feb 2026 with 16 brand partners, covers X, YouTube and TikTok, and uses a tier-based model. Brands post campaigns, creators apply and quote a price, and Kaito screens creators on follower count, reach and impressions. It "need not disclose reasons for rejection." Kaito Pro starts at **$833/mo** (as of the May 7, 2026 article). ([CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/learn/what-is-kaito-earn-yap-points)). Confidence: **medium-high**.
  - Katalyst (July 29, 2026) pays creators on clicks, sign-ups and deposits. 80% of the pool goes to creators. X supplies data "under a data agreement." ([OurCryptoTalk](https://ourcryptotalk.com/news/kaito-katalyst-creator-rewards)). Confidence: medium.
  - The Studio page is behind a login. Tagline: "Where creators and brands grow together." ([kaito.ai/studio](https://www.kaito.ai/studio))
  - Complaints: Studio looks like "a closed marketing agency, where rewards may keep going to the same group of creators," and creators report reward delays. ([KuCoin community](https://www.kucoin.com/news/community/KAITO/6ac7b06a38a26400079330d1), via search). Confidence: low.
- **TwitterScore.** It rates crypto X accounts 0–1000 "by follower quality, not follower count" and claims "300+ VCs & crypto projects." Consumer PRO is **$19/mo** ($228/yr, 7-day card trial; one checkout screen showed $29). The API runs **$49 / $99 / $199 / custom**. ([twitterscore.io](https://twitterscore.io/), [prices](https://twitterscore.io/api/prices)). Confidence: medium.
- **Ethos Network.** Onchain credibility scores built from vouches, reviews and slashes, staked in ETH or WHUF, on Base. A Chrome extension shows scores on X (v1.13.5, updated Mar 9, 2026). Tagline: "Reputation & credibility for crypto, driven by peer-to-peer reviews & secured by staked Ethereum." Critics flag vouch-for-vouch inflation. No user counts were shown. ([app.ethos.network](https://app.ethos.network/), [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/309231/ethos-network-on-chain-reputation-trust), [Blockworks filing](https://blockworks.com/token-transparency/filing/ethos)). Confidence: medium.
- **Lever.io.** Calls itself the "First Data Driven KOL Marketplace." It covers X, YouTube and TikTok. Brands search and filter, or Lever matches for them. They claim 500+ verified Web3 influencers and 300+ brands. Pricing is custom. ([lever.io](https://lever.io/)). Confidence: low-medium (self-reported).
- **Cookie3 / Cookie DAO.** Cookie DAO shut Snaps after the X ban. It said "KOL Intelligence" was unaffected. By Aug 2026, CoinMarketCap describes a pivot to AI-agent infrastructure. ([CMC](https://coinmarketcap.com/fr/cmc-ai/cookie/latest-updates/), via search). Confidence: **low**. Status unclear.
- **Snaplama X Creators Marketplace** (Nov 2025). Brands buy credits, payment is held in escrow, and creators are "verified and vetted." There are no real numbers on the page. ([Snaplama](https://www.snaplama.com/blog/introducing-worlds-first-x-creators-marketplace-snaplama)). Confidence: low.
- **Kolscan.** A free board that ranks Solana KOL wallets by PnL. One unverified source says Pump.fun bought it in mid-2025. ([Kalkine](https://kalkinemedia.com/education/guides/kolscan-how-solanas-smart-money-gets-ranked), via search). It is onchain reputation for trader-creators, not brand fit. Confidence: low.

## So what for BrandOS

Phase 1 (Oct 23 – Nov 1) is Problem and Proof posts with no selling. This sweep hands you real, citable material.

**Problem posts (all facts sourced above):**

- "x built a tool to match brands with niche creators. you can't apply to it." (Creator Connect is brand-side and managed.) The reply: the Index is the opt-in version.
- "kaito studio launched with 16 brands and a tier system. if you're not in a tier, you're invisible." (CoinGecko on Studio; rejection reasons not disclosed.)
- "crypto KOL agencies sell 'vetted lists of 5000+ KOLs'. none of them publish how they vet." (FORKOFF, KolHQ.)

**Proof angle.** Every competitor scores **influence or authenticity**: follower quality (TwitterScore), vouches (Ethos), authority/authenticity (Favikon). Nobody publishes a **brand-fit read** (archetype, what a brand should know). Teardowns should lead with that difference: "follower quality tells a brand you're real. it doesn't tell them you fit."

**DM targets (brand side, from Nov 9).** Mid-size KOL agencies that sell shortlists (KolHQ and Growgami types) are natural Index browsers. They already sell "find the right KOL" and have to justify their picks. Also aim at creators who got no Kaito Studio tier. (**Inference**; untested.)

**Index features this evidence supports:**

1. **Show the method.** A public "how the brand read works" page. The top criticism of every agency and score is an unpublished method.
2. **Rates field (optional, creator-entered).** KolHQ shortlists and Passionfroot media kits both lead with price. "Price it" is a job no X-native index does in the open.
3. **Disclosure signal.** If BrandOS can detect X's Paid Partnership label on posts, "discloses paid posts" becomes a trust signal for brands. Whether SocialData exposes the label is unknown (Gaps).
4. **Verified should be more than "real."** X's "About this account" and Favikon's authenticity score already cover "is this real." Tie Verified to the brand read plus the audience check, and later to receipts. That matches the track-record plan.

**Pricing evidence (creator side, monthly, as of 2026-10-23):**

- TwitterScore PRO $19
- Favikon Creator €29, Creator Plus €99
- SuperX $49–$199
- Typefully $12.50–$39 (third party)

$29–$39 sits inside the band. Favikon's €29 is the closest like-for-like and has a 14-day card trial.

Brand side:

- Favikon €159–€499+
- Modash $199+
- Kaito Pro from $833
- KOL agencies about $5K+/mo

A paid brand tier later has room to price well above creator Pro.

## Challenges a decision (only if the evidence does)

The evidence is weak, and it touches **"Free to be listed. Pro to stand out"**, specifically the "brands that viewed you" upgrade trigger. That trigger only works if brands **browse**. The two strongest moves in this market went the other way:

- X's Creator Connect is AI-matched and managed: brands give a brief and X picks.
- Kaito Studio is brand-posts-a-campaign: creators apply and brands pick from applicants.

The KOL agencies sell done-for-you shortlists. **Inference:** in crypto, brands may prefer to be handed a shortlist rather than browse a grid. That would mean fewer "a brand viewed you" moments than the plan assumes. The decision stands. One cheap check: ask the 15 brand marketers on Oct 23's DM list "would you browse a list, or want a shortlist sent to you?"

## Gaps

- **Is paid crypto promotion allowed on X today?** The sources conflict (March–May 2026). To verify, open help.x.com's Paid Partnerships policy in a logged-out browser and read the prohibited-industries list and regional notes. This decides whether crypto brands can legally pay Index creators on X outside the EU, UK and Australia.
- **Creator Connect's status since May.** Is it live, self-serve, or US-only? Can creators opt in now? Check @XBusiness posts and business.x.com; both blocked our fetch.
- **Kaito Studio numbers.** Creator count, tiers, payouts. Gated behind login; ask a creator who's in it.
- **Favikon's X depth.** Is the authenticity score live for all X accounts, and does it cover crypto niches beyond the 2025 top-20 list? A free brand account would answer this, but signing up is out of scope for this agent.
- **HypeAuditor's X coverage and price.** Only third-party sources so far.
- **Substitutes evidence.** I found no primary sources on Telegram KOL groups or spreadsheet sourcing. The demand run, or a few brand DMs, should cover this.
- **SocialData and the Paid Partnership label.** Does the data BrandOS pulls include the label? Engineering can check one labelled post.
- **Ethos traction.** No live user or stake numbers were visible.
