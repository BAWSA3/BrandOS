# Demand signals

What creators, SMMs/agencies and brand marketers say they need, in public.
Kept by the `market-research` agent, newest first. Real BrandOS users' words go
in `docs/CUSTOMER-VOICE.md` instead.

Last updated: 2026-10-23 (first run, brief: `docs/research/2026-10-23-demand.md`).

**How to read the rows**

- X post dates marked `~` are worked out from the post ID, because x.com blocks direct reads. Quotes come from the search index and may be cut off.
- Reddit, G2 and Capterra were not readable this run, so those voices are missing (see the brief's Gaps).
- **older** means more than 12 months before the run date.

**Pattern notes (2026-10-23)**

- **Get trusted** is the strongest pattern. It shows up across job posts, brand reviews on three different marketplaces, crypto marketers, and a creator admitting fake growth would go unnoticed. It also runs the other way: creators can't trust projects to pay (35% say every project paid them).
- **Get found** in crypto has a dated cause. X banned pay-to-post apps on Jan 15, 2026, Kaito Yaps closed, and small accounts say Kaito Studio isn't for them.
- Two counter-signals say brands, not creators, are the scarce side.
