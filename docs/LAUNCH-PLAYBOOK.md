# BrandOS Creator Index + Pro: launch playbook

**Launch tier:** Tier 1 (company-defining) · **DRI:** Bawsa · **Build:** Claude
**Public launch:** Mon Nov 2, 2026 · **Close:** Thu Dec 3, 2026 (Founding 100 deadline)
**Status:** Phase 0 (readiness) · Written Oct 22, 2026

Run like a large-company launch: one written brief everyone works from, a positioning
document before any copy, staged rollout with go/no-go gates, a metric tree with
guardrails, a launch-day war room, a weekly business review, and a written retrospective.
This supersedes `docs/MARKETING-STRATEGY.md` as the strategy. `docs/GTM-OPERATING-PLAN.md`
stays the daily calendar and `docs/LAUNCH-PLAN.md` stays the source of truth for the offer,
Stripe and email rules.

## 1. Launch brief (the one page)

|                            |                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Product**                | The BrandOS Creator Index (a public book of creators with a real brand read behind each one) and BrandOS Pro (what makes a creator stand out in it)                        |
| **Customer**               | Crypto creators and founder-creators on X, 5k–100k followers, who want brand deals                                                                                         |
| **Problem**                | Creators are invisible to brands. Brands can't find them, and when they do, follower counts don't tell them who to trust                                                   |
| **Why now**                | Brands are moving budget to creators but are getting burned by inflated audiences; creators have no neutral way to prove their brand                                       |
| **Why us**                 | BrandOS already reads a creator's brand from their real posts (score, archetype, phases) and tracks growth daily. 31k followers see Bawsa as the branding person in crypto |
| **Offer**                  | Free to join and be listed. Free full access until Dec 3. Founding 100: add a card to keep Pro at $29/mo for life (charged Dec 3). $39 after 100                           |
| **North-star metric**      | **Cards added by Dec 3: 40** (commit), 60 (stretch)                                                                                                                        |
| **Launch is a success if** | ≥ 40 cards, ≥ 70% of them still active after the Dec 3 charge, refund/complaint rate < 5%                                                                                  |
| **Launch is a failure if** | < 15 cards by Nov 22 (triggers the pivot review in section 9)                                                                                                              |

## 2. PR/FAQ (written as if it's launch day)

> **BrandOS opens the Creator Index: a public book of crypto creators, with the brand read brands actually need**
>
> _Nov 2, 2026._ Today BrandOS opened the Creator Index, a free, public directory of crypto
> creators where every listing is backed by a BrandOS brand read: a score, an archetype and
> what the creator is known for, read from their real posts rather than follower counts.
> Creators join by signing in with X; brands browse it free.
>
> "Good creators lose deals they never hear about, because brands can't tell them apart from
> inflated accounts," said Bawsa, founder of BrandOS. "The Index gives every creator a page a
> brand can trust, and gives brands a way to find people by what they actually stand for."
>
> Members who want to stand out can go Pro: featured placement, a full profile with score
> history, a list of the brands that viewed them, and from Nov 9 the BrandOS Verified badge.
> The first 100 members to add a card keep Pro at $29 a month for life; nothing is charged
> until Dec 3.
>
> Join at mybrandos.app/index.

**External FAQ**

- _Is it free?_ Yes. Joining, being listed and the full product are free until Dec 3. After
  that, the listing stays free; Pro is paid.
- _Who can join?_ Anyone with a public X account who signs in and chooses to be listed.
- _Can I leave?_ Yes, one click; your listing disappears immediately.
- _Do brands really browse it?_ Brands are being invited personally from Nov 9 and browse free.
  We'll publish real numbers, not promises.
- _How is the score calculated?_ From your recent posts: consistency, clarity, voice and
  engagement quality. The same method for everyone; it's explained on every profile.
- _What does "for life" mean?_ Your $29 never goes up while you stay subscribed.

**Internal FAQ (the hard questions; answer them before a customer does)**

- _What if brands don't show up?_ Then "brands viewed you" stays empty and Pro's best reason
  disappears. Mitigation: Bawsa onboards 5 brands/agencies a day from Nov 9; the Pro pitch
  leads with Featured + Verified + the teardown, which don't depend on brands.
- _What if the Index looks empty on day one?_ It won't: Phase 1 seeds it with 50+ members
  before the public opens it (section 4).
- _Why would someone pay before Dec 3 when everything's free?_ They aren't paying; they're
  locking a price that's visibly going up, with a numbered seat. The deadline is real.
- _Can we afford it if it goes viral?_ Index joins reuse scans (≤ $0.015 each). The SocialData
  balance must be topped up before Phase 1 (risk R1).
- _What's the downside of a public list of people?_ Opt-in only, leave any time, nothing listed
  that isn't already public on X, terms and privacy updated before launch (section 6).

## 3. Positioning and messaging

**Positioning statement**
For **crypto creators who want brand deals**, who are **invisible to the brands that should be
paying them**, **BrandOS** is the **creator index with a real brand read behind every
listing**. Unlike **follower counts and attention leaderboards**, BrandOS shows **what a creator
stands for and how consistently they show up**, so brands can pick with confidence and
creators get chosen for their brand, not their size.

**Messaging house**

| Roof (the one line)       | "Free to be listed. Pro to be picked."                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pillar 1: **Be seen**     | A public page and Index listing brands can find by niche and archetype                                                                            |
| Pillar 2: **Be believed** | A brand read from real posts; Verified badge; growth tracked daily                                                                                |
| Pillar 3: **Be picked**   | Featured placement; "brands that viewed you"; a personal teardown from Bawsa                                                                      |
| Proof points              | Real scans (5.4K in the soft launch week), 1,010 on the list, live teardowns, Index member count, real brand views (publish only once they exist) |
| Tone                      | Lowercase, direct, specific, never hype. Numbers only when they're real                                                                           |

**Competitive frame** (use to sharpen, not to attack; verify any claim before using it in public):

| Alternative                                      | What it measures                   | BrandOS difference                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Follower count                                   | Size                               | Size isn't trust; inflated audiences are common         |
| Attention / mindshare leaderboards               | How much you're talked about       | Volume, not brand fit. BrandOS reads what you stand for |
| Self-reported creator directories and media kits | What creators say about themselves | BrandOS reads it from real posts                        |
| Agencies                                         | Who they already know              | The Index is open and free to join                      |

## 4. Rollout: phases and go/no-go gates

Every phase has entry criteria, what happens, and exit criteria. **No phase starts until the
previous gate is passed.** If a gate fails, the phase extends; the next date moves.

### Phase 0: Readiness (Fri Oct 23 → Wed Oct 28)

- Build the Index MVP + Pro perks (behind `INDEX_ENABLED`); "run 044"; promote to prod.
- Instrument the funnel (section 7). Top up SocialData. Live payment check (Oct 28).
- Problem-first content starts (GTM plan phase 1).
- **Gate 0 (Wed Oct 28):** all readiness items in section 6 green; a real card charges and
  refunds in live mode; scans work on prod.

### Phase 1: Private beta, "early numbers" (Thu Oct 29 → Sun Nov 1)

- Turn the Index on for an allowlist: everyone who replied "index"/"ACCESS", the most active
  list members, and 10 creators Bawsa invites personally. Target **50 members.**
- **Open Founding seats to the beta.** Launch day then opens with real social proof
  ("31 founding members already") instead of zero. This is the single biggest de-risking move.
- Watch 3 people join on a call; fix every point of friction the same day.
- **Gate 1 (Sun Nov 1, 18:00):** ≥ 40 members, ≥ 5 cards added, join flow completed by ≥ 80% of
  people who start it, zero P0 bugs, error rate normal.

### Phase 2: Public launch, "Get listed" (Mon Nov 2 → Sun Nov 8)

- Index public. Launch thread pinned, launch email, war room (section 8).
- Daily member spotlights; Thursday X Space with live teardowns.
- **Gate 2 (Sun Nov 8):** ≥ 200 members, ≥ 10 cards. Below that, run the section 9 playbook
  before Phase 3.

### Phase 3: Growth, "Get verified" → "Get seen" (Mon Nov 9 → Sun Nov 22)

- Verified badge (Pro only) and its campaign. Brand side opens: invited brands browse free.
- "Brands viewed you" live. First brand intro through the Index → case post.
- **Gate 3 (Sun Nov 22):** ≥ 25 cards. Below 15 → pivot review (section 9).

### Phase 4: Close, "Lock it" (Mon Nov 23 → Thu Dec 3)

- Real seat counter daily; Black Friday "no sale" post; Cyber Monday + Dec 2 deadline emails.
- Dec 3: first charges; switch to the paid trial; thank founding members.

### Phase 5: Retrospective (Fri Dec 4 → Mon Dec 7)

- Written launch review (section 10). Decide what Pro costs, which levers to keep, and whether
  to relaunch the parked /bots check as part of Verified.

## 7. Metrics: tree, targets and guardrails

**North star:** cards added (Founding members).

```
Cards added (40)
├── Index members without a card (500) × member → card rate (8%)
│   ├── Signups (650) × signup → join rate (75%)
│   │   └── Scans (2,000) × scan → signup rate (30%)
│   └── Pro visibility: Featured views, Verified, brand views
└── Direct asks: DMs to active members × DM → card rate
```

**Weekly targets** (commit / stretch):

| Week ending  | Members | Cards       | Scans |
| ------------ | ------- | ----------- | ----- |
| Nov 1 (beta) | 50      | 5 / 10      | 300   |
| Nov 8        | 200     | 10 / 15     | 800   |
| Nov 15       | 300     | 18 / 28     | 1,200 |
| Nov 22       | 400     | 25 / 40     | 1,600 |
| Dec 3        | 500     | **40 / 60** | 2,000 |

**Leading indicators (check daily):** DMs sent and reply rate, post replies, handles dropped,
join-flow completion rate, members who come back 3+ times.

**Guardrails (if one trips, stop and fix before pushing harder):**

| Guardrail                            | Threshold             |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Error rate on scan / join / checkout | > 2% of attempts      |
| SocialData balance                   | < $3                  |
| Unsubscribe rate per email           | > 1%                  |
| Index removal ("remove me") rate     | > 10% of members      |
| Card cancellations before Dec 3      | > 20% of cards        |
| Complaints (spam, privacy)           | any pattern, even 2–3 |

**Instrumentation (Phase 0):** `src/lib/analytics.ts` exists (Vercel + PostHog) but has no
launch-funnel events. Add: `scan_completed`, `signup_completed`, `index_join_started`,
`index_joined`, `index_left`, `pro_checkout_started`, `card_added`, plus UTM capture on
signup. Plus `scripts/launch-metrics.mjs`: one command that prints the funnel from the
database (works even if PostHog isn't configured).

## 9. Risk register

| #   | Risk                                                  | Likelihood | Impact   | Mitigation                                                               | Owner       |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------- |
| R1  | SocialData out of credit (happened Oct 23)            | High       | Critical | Top up before Gate 0; auto top-up on; alerts; Index reuses scans         | 👤          |
| R2  | Index looks empty at launch                           | Medium     | High     | Phase 1 private beta seeds 50+ members                                   | 👤          |
| R3  | Brands don't browse → "brands viewed you" stays empty | High       | High     | 5 brand DMs/day from Nov 9; lead Pro with Featured + Verified + teardown | 👤          |
| R4  | Live payments fail                                    | Low        | Critical | Oct 28 live check; Stripe webhook alerts                                 | 👤 + Claude |
| R5  | Founder bandwidth: DMs, replies, calls don't scale    | High       | Medium   | Templates, batching, drafted replies; cap calls at 4/day                 | 👤 + Claude |
| R6  | X limits reach of link posts / DM volume              | Medium     | Medium   | Links in replies; personal DMs only, no bulk; spread DMs through the day | 👤          |
| R7  | Privacy concern about a public list                   | Low        | High     | Opt-in only, one-click removal, terms updated, nothing non-public shown  | Claude      |
| R8  | Scores feel wrong to a big creator, public pushback   | Medium     | Medium   | Show the method; respond calmly with their real data; offer a teardown   | 👤          |
| R9  | Cards added, then cancelled before Dec 3              | Medium     | High     | Onboarding call + teardown delivered before Dec 3; value emails          | 👤          |

**Pivot playbook (if Gate 2 or Gate 3 misses):**

1. Members high, cards low → the Pro difference isn't felt. Ship the next Pro perk early,
   lead every post with Featured/Verified, DM every member personally.
2. Members low → the problem framing isn't landing. Run 3 days of teardowns only (proof gets
   replies), A/B two launch-thread hooks, recheck the join flow on a call.
3. Both low by Nov 22 → hold the price, extend nothing, and use the last 10 days for 30
   customer calls to find what would make them pay. The Dec 3 deadline stays.
