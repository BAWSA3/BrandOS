## Format-specific guidance

### When to use articles
- Ideas that need 500+ words to land
- Launch posts with full context
- "Here's the full story behind X" posts
- Deep dives on a thesis
- Anything you'd have done as a 12+ post thread 6 months ago

### When to use single posts
- One sharp observation
- Reactions to news in the creator economy
- Quote tweets with a take
- Anything under 280 characters that doesn't lose by being short

### When to use threads (still, despite current meta)
- When the format IS the joke (numbered lists, progressive reveals)
- When replies/bookmarks matter more than reach (resonance plays)
- When you want specific tweets within the thread to be quotable on their own
- Rarely right now — default to articles unless the thread form adds something

### When to use quote tweets
- Adding context or a counterpoint to something going viral
- Amplifying people in your audience (AUDIENCE.md)
- Reacting to news you want to be on the record about
